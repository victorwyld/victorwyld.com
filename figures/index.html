Figures

A visual reference to the architecture of Scrutinized Awareness Theory.

These figures illustrate the conceptual architecture of Scrutinized Awareness Theory (SAT), Victor Wyld’s 2026 positive threshold theory of consciousness.

The theory is developed across On the Question of Scrutinized Awareness, The Recompression Problem, The Participanthood Problem, and The Acquisition Problem. Together, the figures map SAT’s consciousness threshold and its broader architecture of scrutiny, implication, carrying, recompression, participation, identity, recognition, and acquisition.
