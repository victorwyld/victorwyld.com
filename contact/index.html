Victor Wyld

Victor Wyld is a contemporary Canadian philosopher and author whose work addresses consciousness, subjective experience, Ownership, participation, recognition, and the relationship between occurrences and those to whom they belong.

Correspondence concerning his philosophical work, academic discussion, publications, interviews, and speaking engagements is welcome.

Writing and publications

Wyld's philosophical papers are available through Zenodo and PhilPapers. The complete five-paper sequence, including The Ownership Answer: What It Is Like, is presented under Work.

Speaking and discussion

Topics of philosophical interest include Scrutinized Awareness Theory, the Hard Problem of consciousness, objective Ownership, sensation and subjectivity, participanthood, artificial intelligence, institutional classification, and recognition.
