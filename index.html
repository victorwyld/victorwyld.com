Victor Wyld

Contemporary philosopher of mind and consciousness

In 2026, Wyld developed Scrutinized Awareness Theory, proposing that awareness becomes consciousness when it becomes something the participant must carry. His five-paper sequence continues through The Recompression Problem, The Participanthood Problem, and The Acquisition Problem, culminating in The Ownership Answer: What It Is Like. The fifth paper distinguishes immediate, pre-conscious Ownership from consciousness and challenges the inference that the explanatory gap requires additional phenomenal properties or fundamental laws.
